Filters

Back to Credentials Knowledge Base

Where: Credential sidebar > Filters (/credential/filters)

Filters let visitors search for credentials. Toggle each filter on or off, then select Save.

Group Meaning
Departments Academic units in your area (official or informal).
Skills/Topics What students expect to gain. Student-focused; may span departments.
Lengths Expected time to complete. Select all that apply if there are several options.
Tags Defined by your college or department, such as self-paced degrees or certifications.

Groups with no filters are not shown. If your source administrator added instructions, they appear under Instructions.

A program's Filter List is compiled from the filters on its credentials, so edit filters here.

Messages

  • No filters are available for this source: add them in Configuration > Filters.
  • Filters are not currently being used for this source: enable them in Configuration > Fields Used > Credentials.
