# All Programs and Credentials

[Back to Audit Reports Knowledge Base](README.md)

**Where:** Audit > **All Programs and Credentials** (`/audit/allprograms`)

**Description:** Lists every program and credential in your source and lets you download the list as a tab-delimited text file.

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Title** | The program or credential title. |
| **Url** | The item's identifier or address as shown by the report. |
| **Is Active** | Whether the item is active on your site. |

## Download
Select **Download as Tab-delimed Text File** to save the list. Open it in a spreadsheet or text editor.
