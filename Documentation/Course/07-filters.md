# Filters

[Back to Courses Knowledge Base](README.md)

**Where:** Course sidebar > **Filters** (`/course/filters`)

Toggle filters on or off so visitors can search for the course.

| Group | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Departments** | Academic units in your area (official or informal). |
| **Skills/Topics** | What students expect to gain; student-focused. |
| **Lengths** | Expected time to take the course, from about an hour to a full semester. |
| **Tags** | Defined by your college or department. |

## Buttons
- **Save**: stores your choices.
- **Autogenerate**: shown only in some cases; generates filter choices automatically. Review the result, then save.
- **Back**: returns to the page you came from, when you arrived from a quick link.

## Messages
- **No filters are available for this source**: add them in **Configuration > Filters**.
- **Filters are not currently being used**: enable them in **Configuration > Fields Used > Courses**.
