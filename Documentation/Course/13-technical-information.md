# Technical Information

[Back to Courses Knowledge Base](README.md)

**Where:** Course sidebar > **Technical Information** (`/course/technical`)

- **Status on Site**: whether the course is active on your site.
- **Internal Search Text**: text to help editors find the course; not for display.
- **Save** stores changes.

## Information to Copy
- **Id**: the course's unique ID.
- **Edit Link**: direct link to edit it.

## Delete
The delete notice at the bottom removes the course. Deletion cannot be undone.

A **Back** button appears when you arrived from a quick link.
