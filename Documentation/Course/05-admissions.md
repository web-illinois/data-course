**Where:** Course sidebar > **Admissions** (`/course/admissions`)

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Admission Requirements** | What students need to enroll. |
| **Transfer Student Requirements** | Requirements for transfer students. |
| **Program Tracks** | Tracks or options related to the course. |

Select **Save** when done.
