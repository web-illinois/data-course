# Transcript Information

[Back to Credentials Knowledge Base](README.md)

**Where:** Credential sidebar > **Transcript Information** (`/credential/transcriptable`)

## Fields
| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Is This Credential Transcriptable** | Whether the credential appears on a transcript. |
| **Transcriptable Name** | Name as it appears on the transcript. |
| **Major Title** | Major title. |
| **Minor Title** | Minor title. |
| **Disclaimer Text** | Disclaimer shown with the credential. |

Select **Save** when finished.
